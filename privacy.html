Privacy Policy

Effective October 26, 2026

This policy explains what information Cut Engine ("we") collects when you connect a social media account, how it is used, and how you can remove it. Cut Engine is a personal editing tool used by the creator of the @togi.cuts account.

Information we collect

  • Basic account information from TikTok and other platforms you connect, such as Instagram: your account ID, display name and profile picture.
  • Access tokens that let Cut Engine act on your account with the permissions you approve.
  • Your posted videos and their statistics, such as view, like, comment and share counts.
  • Video clips that Cut Engine creates and sends to your account.

We do not collect your password, contacts, messages, location or payment information.

How we use it

  • To send finished clips to your TikTok drafts or publish them to accounts you connect.
  • To show how your posted videos perform so you can decide what to make next.

We do not use your information for advertising and we do not sell it.

Sharing

We do not sell, rent or share your information with anyone. Information is only sent to the platform it came from (for example, a clip sent to TikTok) when you ask Cut Engine to do so.

Storage and security

Information is stored on the operator's own computer and accounts, with access limited to the operator. Access tokens are kept private and are never published or shared.

Keeping and deleting your information

We keep information only while your account is connected. You can disconnect Cut Engine at any time from the app permissions section of your TikTok or Instagram settings. To have all stored information deleted, email us and we will delete it within 30 days.

Children

Cut Engine is not intended for anyone under 18, and we do not knowingly collect information from minors.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the date at the top of this page.

Contact

Email togiii.aura@gmail.com with any privacy questions or deletion requests.

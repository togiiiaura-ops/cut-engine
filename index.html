Cut Engine

Cut Engine is a personal video editing tool used by the creator of the @togi.cuts account. It turns long videos into short vertical clips with captions, then sends each finished clip to the creator's own TikTok drafts.

Privacy Policy Terms of Service

How it works

  1. The creator signs in with TikTok to connect their own account.
  2. Cut Engine edits a clip and sends it to the creator's TikTok inbox as a draft.
  3. The creator opens the draft in the TikTok app, reviews it, edits the caption, adds a sound, and posts it themselves.
  4. Cut Engine shows view, like and share counts for the creator's posted videos so they can see which edits perform best.

What it does not do

Cut Engine does not post anything without the creator's review, does not sell or share data, and is not offered to other users.
